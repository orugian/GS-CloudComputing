POLICY SUMMARY. IceMan policies: 2 year manufacturer warranty on every watch. 30 day return if unused. Free authenticity certificate included. Free engraving for purchases above BRL 50000. Trade-in accepted with free evaluation. Payment via Pix or debit card gets 5 percent discount.

PAYMENT POLICY. IceMan accepts Pix and debit card with 5 percent discount. Credit card accepted at full price. Credit card in up to 6 installments with zero interest. From 7 to 12 installments a monthly interest of 1.5 percent applies. External financing available via Banco Safra and Itau Personnalite.

WARRANTY POLICY. IceMan provides 2 year manufacturer warranty on every watch sold. Returns and exchanges accepted within 30 days if watch is unused and in original packaging. Free watch revision during first 2 years. Free preventive maintenance every year for registered customers. Certified technical support for all five brands.

SERVICE POLICY. IceMan offers authorized technical service for all 5 brands sold. Every watch includes a free authenticity certificate. Engraving service costs BRL 150. Engraving is free for purchases above BRL 50000 and takes 5 business days. Watch reservation held for 48 hours with customer registration. Longer reservations require 30 percent deposit. Stock transfers between IceMan Ibirapuera and IceMan Paulista take 5 business days.
